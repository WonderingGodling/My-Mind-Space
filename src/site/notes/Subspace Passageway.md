# <center><span style="color:#FEDCBA">Subspace Passageway</span></center>



There Are Multiple Subspace Passageways (Or “Subways”) In Harmony. These Are Supposedly Deformations Of Space That Were Formed When The Gods Hastily Uprooted The World Tree From This Realm. The Scars Of Space That Were Left Behind, Though Dangerous, Proved To Be Very Useful As A Way Of Connecting Distant Nations Together Which Is Why Each Capital Was Carefully Built Around The Entrances And Exits To These Spaces.

  

## Usage

Subways Are Very Popular Modes Of Transportation But They Are unfortunately Dangerous. For This Reason Every Subway Has A Guard And An Outpost. What Do They Do? Well The Outpost Is In Charge Of Administration. In Order To Be Granted Passage Into The Subway System You Need To Declare That You Understand The Risks You Are Taking (GIving You A Wooden Coin That You Must Show To The Guard For Entry). The Outpost Is Also In Charge Of Arranging For You An Escort, Providing Some Magical Lights, Giving You A Chocolate Bar Perhaps. They Are Used To Patrons With Deep Pockets So They Are Capable Of Carrying Out Most Tasks.
