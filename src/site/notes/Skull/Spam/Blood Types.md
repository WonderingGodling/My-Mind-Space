### **Detailed Breakdown Of ABO Blood Types**

#### **1. A+**
- **A antigen** present on red blood cells and the **Rh factor** is present (positive).

#### **2. A-**
- **A antigen** present on red blood cells but no **Rh factor** (negative).

#### **3. B+**
- **B antigen** present on red blood cells and the **Rh factor** is present (positive).

#### **4. B-**
- **B antigen** present on red blood cells but no **Rh factor** (negative).

#### **5. AB+**
- Both **A and B antigens** present on red blood cells and the **Rh factor** is present (positive).

#### **6. AB-**
- Both **A and B antigens** present on red blood cells but no **Rh factor** (negative).

#### **7. O+**
- Neither **A** nor **B antigens** present on red blood cells, but the **Rh factor** is present (positive).

#### **8. O-**
- Neither **A** nor **B antigens** present on red blood cells and no **Rh factor** (negative).

---

#### **9. A1+**
- **A1 antigen** is strongly expressed, with the **Rh factor** present.  
- Most common expression of the **A antigen**.

#### **10. A1-**
- **A1 antigen** is strongly expressed, but no **Rh factor** is present.

#### **11. A2+**
- **A2 antigen** is expressed, but at a weaker level than **A1**, with the **Rh factor** present.

#### **12. A2-**
- **A2 antigen** is expressed, but at a weaker level than **A1**, with no **Rh factor** present.

#### **13. A3+**
- **A3 antigen** is a rare variant of the **A antigen** with the **Rh factor** present.

#### **14. A3-**
- **A3 antigen** is a rare variant of the **A antigen** with no **Rh factor**.

#### **15. Ax+**
- Extremely weak expression of **A antigen**, sometimes mistaken for **O**, with **Rh factor** present.

#### **16. Ax-**
- Extremely weak expression of **A antigen**, sometimes mistaken for **O**, with no **Rh factor**.

#### **17. Am+**
- **Minimal expression** of **A antigen**, often leading to it being mistaken for **O**, with **Rh factor** present.

#### **18. Am-**
- **Minimal expression** of **A antigen**, often leading to it being mistaken for **O**, with no **Rh factor**.

#### **19. Aend+**
- **Extremely weak expression** of **A antigen**, often requiring special tests to detect, with **Rh factor** present.

#### **20. Aend-**
- **Extremely weak expression** of **A antigen**, often requiring special tests to detect, with no **Rh factor**.

---

#### **21. B3+**
- **B3 antigen** is a rare expression of the **B antigen**, with **Rh factor** present.

#### **22. B3-**
- **B3 antigen** is a rare expression of the **B antigen**, with no **Rh factor**.

#### **23. Bx+**
- Very weak expression of the **B antigen**, with **Rh factor** present.

#### **24. Bx-**
- Very weak expression of the **B antigen**, with no **Rh factor**.

#### **25. Bm+**
- **Minimal expression** of the **B antigen**, leading to confusion with **O**, with **Rh factor** present.

#### **26. Bm-**
- **Minimal expression** of the **B antigen**, leading to confusion with **O**, with no **Rh factor**.

#### **27. Bel+**
- **Bel antigen** is a rare expression of the **B antigen**, with **Rh factor** present.

#### **28. Bel-**
- **Bel antigen** is a rare expression of the **B antigen**, with no **Rh factor**.

---

#### **29. A1B+**
- **A1** and **B antigens** are both present with **Rh factor**.

#### **30. A1B-**
- **A1** and **B antigens** are both present with no **Rh factor**.

#### **31. A2B+**
- **A2** and **B antigens** are both present with **Rh factor**.

#### **32. A2B-**
- **A2** and **B antigens** are both present with no **Rh factor**.

#### **33. A3B+**
- **A3** and **B antigens** are both present with **Rh factor**.

#### **34. A3B-**
- **A3** and **B antigens** are both present with no **Rh factor**.

#### **35. AxB+**
- **Ax** and **B antigens** are both present with **Rh factor**.

#### **36. AxB-**
- **Ax** and **B antigens** are both present with no **Rh factor**.

---

#### **37. Oh+ (Bombay phenotype)**
- No **A**, **B**, or **H** antigens are present, but **Rh factor** is present.  
- A very rare and unique blood type.

#### **38. Oh- (Bombay phenotype)**
- No **A**, **B**, or **H** antigens are present, and no **Rh factor** is present.  
- Extremely rare and unique blood type.

### **Detailed Breakdown Of MNS Blood Types**

#### **1. M+N+**
- Both **M** and **N** antigens are present on red blood cells.  
- Common combination of the MNS blood group system.

#### **2. M+N-**
- **M** antigen is present, but **N** antigen is absent.  
- This is a common phenotype.

#### **3. M-N+**
- **N** antigen is present, but **M** antigen is absent.  
- Occurs in a smaller portion of the population.

#### **4. M-N-**
- Neither **M** nor **N** antigens are present.  
- This is a rare or null phenotype in the MNS system.

#### **5. S+**
- Presence of the **S** antigen, one of the secondary antigens in the MNS system.

#### **6. s+**
- Presence of the **s** antigen, another secondary antigen of the MNS system.

#### **7. S-s-**
- Neither **S** nor **s** antigens are present.  
- This is considered a **null phenotype** in the MNS system.

#### **8. M+N+S+**
- Presence of **M**, **N**, and **S** antigens on red blood cells.

#### **9. M+N+S-s-**
- Presence of **M** and **N** antigens, but **S** antigen is present and **s** antigen is absent.

#### **10. M+N-S+s+**
- **M** and **N** antigens are present, but **S** antigen is absent and **s** antigen is present.

#### **11. M-N-S-s-**
- **M** antigen is absent, **N** antigen is present, and **S** and **s** antigens are absent.  
- Rare phenotype in the MNS system.

#### **12. M+N-S+**
- Presence of **M** and **N** antigens, but **S** antigen is present and **s** antigen is absent.

#### **13. M+N-S+s-**
- **M** and **N** antigens present, **S** antigen absent, and **s** antigen present.  
- Relatively uncommon combination.

#### **14. M+N+S+U+**
- Presence of **M**, **N**, **S**, and **U** antigens on red blood cells.  
- **U** antigen is a characteristic of the S-s- phenotype, and its presence can occur in specific combinations.

#### **15. M-N-S-s-U+**
- **M** and **N** antigens are absent, **S** and **s** antigens are present, and **U** antigen is present.  
- A rare and complex combination in the MNS system.

#### **16. Mk+**
- Presence of the **Mk** antigen, a rare antigen within the MNS blood group system.  
- Not commonly encountered.

#### **17. Mk-**
- Absence of the **Mk** antigen.  
- Rare variation related to the MNS system.

#### **18. U+**
- The **U antigen** is present, often found in individuals with the **S-s-** phenotype.  
- Critical for blood transfusion compatibility in rare cases.

#### **19. S-s- (Null Phenotype)**
- Neither **S** nor **s** antigens are present on red blood cells.  
- This is a **null phenotype** in the MNS blood group system, often associated with the **U antigen**.
