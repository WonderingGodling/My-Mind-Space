## History
Year 0: [[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Campaigns/Starfall/Characters/Starfall PCs/Other/mercil cosmia\|mercil cosmia]] makes a deal with a devil in order to be able to bare children, this causes jetty to have demon blood after her birth 
As She Was Growing Up Her Parents Always Described Her Birth As An Interesting Ordeal But They Never Explained Any Struggles They Faced During It


Year 1: born in [[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Realities/Kawshekh/Location/Planets/Harmony/Regions/Krypton/Krypton States/Dolad/Named Towns And Cities/Kent\|Kent]], [[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Realities/Kawshekh/Location/Planets/Harmony/Regions/Krypton/Krypton\|Krypton]], parents were two humans that ran [[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Campaigns/Starfall/Characters/Starfall PCs/Other/cosmia crime family\|the cosmia crime family]], focusing on drug and arms dealing, has an identical twin sister named [[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Campaigns/Starfall/Characters/NPC/Related NPCs/prexa\|prexa]] 

  
Year 8: begins learning chess as well as being introduced to the family enforcer who begins training her in hand to hand combat
"With A Big Enough Hammer Every Problem Looks Like A Nail" Became "If You Dont Like It, Punch It" Which Is A teaching That Surprisingly Got Her Out Of Trouble More Than It Got Her Into It


Year 11: is kicked out of school for fighting, starts to attempt to learn some forms of martial arts
This Is An Example Of Her Getting Into Trouble But When One Door Closes Another Opens


Year 13: begins helping the family business by helping manufacture weaponry  while her sister helps with creation of narcotics 
In [[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Realities/Kawshekh/Location/Planets/Harmony/Regions/Krypton/Krypton\|Krypton]] The Manufacture Of Weaponry Is....Different. Jetty Has An Affinity For Heat And Forging Gunmetal Was Never An Issue Whether A Guns Design Was Changed To Be All Copper Or All Tungsten. The Difficulty Came From Custom Rifling, Alternate Chambering, Or Manipulated Sights.


Year 15: her parents are killed in an organized hit by other crime families, during the chaos her left arm transformed into a demonic counterpart, this transformation killed her attackers allowing her to escape, her sister is taken in and raised up by one of the crime families, her demonic blood awakens as well turning her insides demonic instead of her outward parts, jetty is unaware of her twins survival 

  
Year 16: begins living at a [[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Campaigns/Starfall/Characters/Starfall PCs/Other/Jettys monastery\|monastery]] with monks of the four elements, learns about spiritualism as well as picking up an ethos that strength is the only thing that matters in the world, she also learns to work a tattoo kit using both an automatic tattoo machine and by needling 
The Monastery Is Funded By Someone Outside And So While Living There For A little Over A Year She Has Access To A Great Deal Of Spiritual Training Equipment, Even The tattoo Equipment She Is Used To Works By Smoothing The Vessel Of Ones Spirit To Show Some Of the Art Within
  

Year 17: leaves the monks on good terms then begins traveling, starts taking jobs as an enforcer and hired killer, gets her first tattoo as well as her metal shield gauntlets as well as wearing  a outfit that consists of  chainmail and some basic leather underpieces 
   

Year 18:expands her tattoo, her unwavering belief in her ethos makes her into a paladin, now being infused by radiant energy she becomes a lot more brazen in her fights, on a contract is nearly killed by a targets bodyguard, ends up killing both the target and the guard but is left with multiple broken and fractured bones, this leads to her taking painkillers, smoking and drinking alcohol 
  

Year 19: meets ‘[[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Campaigns/Starfall/Characters/Regalia tomo\|Regalia tomo]]’ a drow assassin, meets during a job gone bad, ends up saving jetty’s life, they travel together through the year taking jobs as a pair at times, overtime they grow to like each other and begin dating  half way into the year, on the last day of the year [[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Campaigns/Starfall/Characters/Regalia tomo\|Regalia tomo]] gifts Jetty a wooden carving she had commissioned of her demonic arm, this is around the time jetty begins calling her ‘[[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Campaigns/Starfall/Characters/Regalia tomo\|Regalia tomo]]’ 


Year 20: works a solo job down south where she encounters [[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Campaigns/Starfall/Characters/Starfall PCs/Group 1/Sera\|Sera]], job goes bad  and they end up in a brawl, ends in a draw with [[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Campaigns/Starfall/Characters/Starfall PCs/Group 1/Sera\|Sera]] having to ditch their current vessel and Jetty bleeding out on the restaurant floor, Jetty heals herself up with her divine powers before making a deal with [[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Campaigns/Starfall/Characters/Starfall PCs/Group 1/Sera\|Sera]] paying ¼ of the contracts cost to go back and kill the original contractor,  half way into the year she gets a job to retrieve an artifact, she is not told much about the item but the pay is in the thousands and it's the kind of job that would shoot any criminal up to the top in the hierarchy, she goes on the job and ends up taking two weeks prepping for it and gathering info,  half way through she meets up with [[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Campaigns/Starfall/Characters/Regalia tomo\|Regalia tomo]] and finds out she's also working the job they decide to work together, when the job finally happens it mostly goes well until by the time they are leaving the  museum that was housing the artifact, [[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Campaigns/Starfall/Characters/Regalia tomo\|Regalia tomo]] ended up betraying her and kneecaps her with a pistol  before running away, jetty is apprehended by the guards and locked away, while awaiting transfer jetty uses her arm to melt away the bars and escape in the night, from here she hides away for the rest of the year, recovering both physically and mentally.
The Artifact, [[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Campaigns/Starfall/Characters/Starfall PCs/Other/The Crystal Globe Of The Fallen God De Skoe\|The Crystal Globe Of The Fallen God De Skoe]], Was Held In A Museum In Lair. It Was Surrounded In One-Way Glass Making It So That (Paying) Visitors Could See It But It Couldnt See Them. The Doors Were Magically Locked, The Guards Were Slacking On But None of This Stopped The Duo.

Year 21: has finished her main tattoo, obtains her current outfit(suit pants, snakeskin shoes, button up, face tattoo and current hair type) has started taking jobs again after her hiatus  and is slowly planning to make her own criminal empire, travels up to [[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Realities/Kawshekh/Location/Planets/Harmony/Regions/Hollywood/Named Cities And Towns/Hell\|Hell]], along the way she tattoos travelers for free, during this her tattoo kit breaks after a mistaken surge of power from her demonic arm caused by thinking back to being betrayed by [[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Campaigns/Starfall/Characters/Regalia tomo\|Regalia tomo]].
