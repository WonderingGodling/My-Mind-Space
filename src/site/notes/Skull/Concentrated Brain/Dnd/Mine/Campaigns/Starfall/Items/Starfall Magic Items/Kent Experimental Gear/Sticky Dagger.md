This Is A Rapier Thin Sword That Is Thickened By An Enveloping Of Glazed Iron Barbs. The Handle Is Uncomfortably Sticky To Hold But It Is Far More Comfortable Than The Spiked Side Of The Sword That Will Hopefully Be used Violently Against Those Who Oppose You

2d6 Piecing Or 1d6 Slashing
All Piercing Damage Dealt By This Dagger Is Doubled
If Piercing Damage Is Dealt Must Roll 2nd To-Hit, If Your Roll Beats The Opponents AC The Dagger Gets Suck In Your Opponent Dealing 2 Piercing Damage Every Ten Seconds
