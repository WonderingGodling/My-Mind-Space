### Bio
A young (early 20’s) and airheaded human paladin raised most of her life as a maid for the Fontaine house, only recently picking up the capability for fighting. Raised on folklore and legends, she has an almost unrealistic adventurous spirit, seeing the opportunity to guard [[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Campaigns/Starfall/Characters/Starfall PCs/Paladin Pals/Everett De La Fontaine\|Everett]] as a way to live out her fantasy of following the footsteps of her heroes.

Extremely chivalrous and knightly approach to everyone, as well as an extremely caring and helpful person, being a lot more casual for those who get close to her, dropping the exalted persona subconsciously. A knight errant passionate about their journey, just as prone to any potential conflict and realizations that the real world is not as simple as she may think.

The First Thing That People Think When They Look At This Young Lady Is "Woah They Look Like They Will Kick My Ass". Sometimes A Blessing, Sometimes A Curse.
People Who Spend Enough Time Surrounded By Servitude Will Be Able To Recognise Sinclair's Experience, Her Posture, Her Demeanour And Her Proximity To Her Liege All Make Her Purpose Evident To Those Who Want To Know.

A Legacy Servant Of The Fontaines, And A Lover Of Folk Lore Had Made Her Not The Most Liked In [[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Realities/Kawshekh/Location/Planets/Harmony/Regions/Gi Fotias/Unnamed Villages/Gi Fotias 25\|The Two-Five]] But Amongst The Most Respected (Aside From Nobility)

### Backstory
The Proud Daughter Of [[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Campaigns/Starfall/Characters/NPC/Related NPCs/Sienna Rosemond\|Sienna]] And  [[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Campaigns/Starfall/Characters/NPC/Related NPCs/Viggo Rosemond\|Viggo]] Rosemond Made Prouder By The Favouritism She Didnt Know She Was Experiencing Growing Up. Everybody Gave Her Space To "Work" On Her Maid Duties More So That They Could Avoid Her Than So That She Could Do The Best Job Possible (Which She Always Tried To Do). 

She Never Had Any Misconceptions Regarding Her Position In The Household Despite Being The Daughter Of A Beloved Maid And The Best Friend Of Earl [[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Campaigns/Starfall/Characters/Starfall PCs/Paladin Pals/Everett De La Fontaine\|Everett]] De La Fontaine. She ~~Knew~~ Believed That Her Place As A Maid Was Always One Mistake Away From Being Revoked So She Put Her All Into Helping Her Friend. For Sinclair There Was No Time To Relax And Have A "Me Day" Or To Put Herself On Top Of The Food Chain But At The Same Time Laying Low Was Just Not An Option She Knew Existed

As The Years Grew So Did The Amount Of Passion She Had For Who She Would Eventually Come To Refer To As "Her Liege". Mostly Fuelled By How Much She Loved Their Time Together And Partially Fuelled By The Stories That Her Mother Used To Tell Her. Stories Of Unlikely Heroes, Caring Gods, And Shining Knights. As More Of Her World Was Filled By These Fantasies As Was More Of Her Perspective.

One Day Something Happened To [[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Campaigns/Starfall/Characters/Starfall PCs/Paladin Pals/Everett De La Fontaine\|Everett]]. Something Similar To [[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Campaigns/Starfall/Characters/Starfall PCs/Paladin Pals/Cirquelle\|Cirquelle]] But More Spontaneous. Something That Sinclair Couldnt Fix....Yet.

[[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Campaigns/Starfall/Characters/NPC/Related NPCs/Renata\|Renata]] Was The Name Of Her Mentor. There Was, As Always, No Time To Mess Around So Sin Put All Of Her Free Time Into Learning How To Be A Guard— A Knight For Her Liege. [[Skull/Concentrated Brain/Dnd/Mine/Campaigns/Starfall/Characters/Starfall PCs/Paladin Pals/Everett De La Fontaine\|Everett]] Had Chosen Her To Accompany Him Alone Across Harmony And She Would Ensure This Quest A Success!
