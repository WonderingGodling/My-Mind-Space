# <center><span style="color:#122430">Twin Sticks</span></center>
![Sprite-0002.png\|110](/img/user/images/Sprite-0002.png)

Two Long Metal Wands, Thirteen Inches In Length Reminiscent Of Knitting Needles. When You Cast Spells With These Wands They Heat Up Pleasantly.

Both Wands Need To Be Held For This To Be Counted As A Spellcasting Focus And For It To Give You Its Effects
Your Proficiency Modifier Is Always Added For Spells Cast With These Wands, If Your Proficiency Modifier Would Have Already Been Added Add It Again
If A Spell Effect Requires An Action After It Has Been Cast, That Effect Now Requires A Free Action
You Can Concentrate On Two Spells At Once
