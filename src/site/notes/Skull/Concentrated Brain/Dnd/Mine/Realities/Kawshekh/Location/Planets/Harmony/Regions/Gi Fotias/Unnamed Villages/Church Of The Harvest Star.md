# <center><span style="color:#FFDCAA">Church Of The Harvest Star</span></center>


## Beliefs
That There Is No Such Thing As Wasted Effort And That It All Holistically Comes Back To You

The Energy That You Put Into Something Is Proportional To The Quality Of The Outcome.
The Energy That You Put Into A Person Effects The How Much That They Put Into Something.
The Energy That You Put Into Yourself Allows You To Invest In Someone Else.

## The Taboos Of The Harvest Star 
Sabotage

## The Social Organisation And Initiations Of The Harvest Star 
Manual Laborers
Practically Minded People
Lineages With Long Lifespans
There Is A Long Initiation Process That Requires Finding A New Project And Completing It Alone (Under Supervision), At The End Of This Process The Project Gets Blessed And Becomes A Physical Representation Of You Faith. Of Course The Longer And Bigger Ones Project (Say A Lighthouse) The Stronger Their Blessing 
## Places Of Worship In The Harvest Star 
Typically A Crop Field With An Intricate Pattern Made Out Of The Tilled Soil, People Who Make These Fields Have To Learn The Pattern That Corresponds To Each Season For The Size Of The Field
