### Legal System
#### Report
Any Crime Detailed To The Proper Authorities Will Be Investigated As Soon As Possible
#### Guards
If  A Guard Is Under The Impression that A Crime Is Being Or Has Been Committed They Have Operational Discresion.
The Guards Are Instructed To Only Take Lethal Action In The case of Self Defence, Otherwise The Accused Is Taken to Court And Put On Trial
#### Trail By Fire
In Front Of Three Judges And A Jury, You Are Put Under The Influence Of Zone Of Truth, Resisting This Would Be Admittance To The Crime
The Zone Of Truth Spell In This Court Does Allow For Lies And Manipulation Of The Truth But At The Cost Of Being Burned According To How Egregious Your Falsehood Was 
### Differences Between Day And Night
#### Day
The Sky, Though Still Blue, Has A warm Tint To It, The Light Fully Stretches Over Everything And Somewhat Around Corners/Under Covers
#### Night
The Stars Are red During The Night. They Are Good For Navigation But Are terrible For Illumination
If Both Moons Are Out The Light Seems To Come From The Ground Instead Of The Sky
