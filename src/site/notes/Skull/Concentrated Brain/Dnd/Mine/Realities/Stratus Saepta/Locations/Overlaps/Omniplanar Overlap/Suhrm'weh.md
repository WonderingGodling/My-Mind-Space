# Suhrm'weh
## Omniplanar Overlap

- Description: This Is The Only Overlap Which Intersects With Every Plane, As A Result It Is A Centre Of Community, Advancement And Politics
- Details:

- Overlaps With: Everywhere
- Governing Structure: Democratic Government Run By Representatives From Each Plane
- Political Power: Highest Political Power

- Landscape: 
	- One Huge City With Large
	- Tall Skyscrapers And Mantions Spread Out Into The Distance Of The Dense City
	- Further From The Centre Of The City: buildings Get Taller And Thinner
	- All Buildings Are Designed To Be Communal
- Time Telling:
	- the Same As The Real World
- Day/Night:
	- Day: 
		- Bright Blue
		- 1 Mobile Sun (Clockwise), 1 Stationary Sun
		- 12 Hours Long
	- Night:
		- Dark Green
		- 1 Mobile Moon (Anticlockwise), 1 Stationary Moon
- Climate
	- The Climate Is Somehow Ideal To Suit All Life With Warm Temperatures Which Do Not Cause Discomfort To Cold Seeking Inhabitants
	- Any Crop Can Grow Either During The Day Or The Night (Depending On Which Plane It Was Imported From)
	- 1 Day Of Boiling Rain A Week
		- 1 Thunder Storm Every 2 Rainstorms
- Animals:
	- All Life Can Be Found Somewhere Here, Just Not In Great Amount
- Plants: 
	- All Life Can Be Found Somewhere Here, Just Not In Great Amount
- Races:

	- Diplomacy and Peace Is Encouraged
	- A Lot Of Movement Between This Overlap And Other Planes As People Seek Both Permanent And Temporary Solace Inside And Outside The City
	- Theres Always A Large Amount Of People Outside
		- Although Everyone Does Have A Place To Stay It Seems As Though Thee Is An Overpopulation Problem
