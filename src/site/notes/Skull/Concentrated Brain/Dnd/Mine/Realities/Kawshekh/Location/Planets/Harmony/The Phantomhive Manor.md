# The Phantomhive Manor
In the depths of a sparse forest, a warm iron gate sticks out of the twinkling black grass. Behind the gate is a mansion made out of faded brown bricks and wood. The Phantomhive Manor is home only to Lord Alan Phantomhive.

## Layout
The double door entrance leads to a large foyer 

### Foyer
The foyer has a fountain in the middle. The fountain has a deep bowl of ice blue water and five metres above the centre, a black orb spouting the water out into the room. The water streams weave around the room and converge back into the bowl.

The foyer has a red carpet which leads around the room, circling the fountain, leading to the exits and traveling up the stairs. Underneath the carpet is a bright white marble in a hash pattern.

Opposite to the doors is a double helix staircase which leads to the second floor and then to the roof. The staircase is made of the same marble but with a wood grain pattern and gold newels.

Between the two bases of the stairs is the single door exit to the garden.

Stage left of the foyer is the dining room then kitchen. 

Stage right of the foyer is the theatre.

### Garden
The garden is home to more of the stargrass, a koi pond and a singular tree with a hammock hanging from one of its branches. 

The perimeter of the garden is lined with a full red to fuchsia hue of colours, each distorted column of roses is a slightly further along wavelength of colour creating a complete colour wheel surrounding whoever is inside the garden.
