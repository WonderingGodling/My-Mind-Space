Ignorance Is Bliss 
Why Can Only Those Who Are 
Dead Know Nothing More?

True Euphoria
Originates From Nothing
Nothing Is Perfect

Nothing Never Needs
Repair Nothing Never Breaks
Nothing Is Happy

Darkness Is Nothing 
Hiding From The Light The Moon
Is The Sun At Night

When One Only Dreams
Of Nothing One Cannot Sleep
