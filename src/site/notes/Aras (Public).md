## Backstory
### The Incident
Aras Was A ranger.

Only A Few Years Ago Him And The Two People That Understood Him Could Be Found Roaming The Woods And Flats Of Gi Fotias. They Would Find Cool Plants, Help Hurt Animals, And Have The Greatest Of Time While Doing It. That Was Until Juul Got Sick.

His childhood friend, another ranger named Beth, fell of a cliff while trying to find Feverfew to cure The Third Member Of Their Little Group From An Illness. He was holding on to her but wasn’t strong enough to pull her up. When he finally got down to the foot of the cliff Beth was already gone. Unfortunately By The Time Aras Got Back It Was Too Late for Juul Too.

He grew up with them and they accepted him for who he was. They were his only friends. He feels he isn’t good enough because of his low empathy, his quiet nature and transness.

He couldn’t accept this and made a pact with a god to teach him how to revive people. God takes the form of a wolf and travels with Aras.

### Recently

He watches over the forest and helps people in need. He has a hard time with feelings and has low empathy. He understands physical problems but not mental ones.

He has a quiet personality and keeps to himself. He is careful with approaching others on a deeper level. He is stubborn and focuses on his goals. He cares for others but has a hard time showing that in ways that aren’t acts of serves. He acts nervous around others and seems like he is always on guard, but does show kindness and a want to help others.

He asks a lot of questions to figure out how people are feeling and is extra kind and sweet to people. This is because he is afraid that people will reject him over an unintentional social mistake, so he tries to build up goodwill. He assumes the worst of peoples actions because he doesn’t realise people may have a reason for what they do that isn’t just to hurt others. He is impatient.

He has a hard time falling in love because he had a crush on one of the friends who died. He is still grieving.

At the moment he is travelling to get medicine, he was send away by the boss because he had an outburst at the new rangers because he felt they were going to replace his friends.

The Celestial doesn’t change much for him since he always was inclined to help others. But it does make him want to go were the other celestials reside. The wolf chose him because he wants to guide him to the right path.
