#### Bacterial engineers ‘ - critically discuss their role in production of functional biomaterials


 Microbes have the ability to produce polymers with diverse structures. They
serve varying roles which help microbes thrive within different ecosystems.
 Many of them are industrially/clinically relevant biomaterials due to their
biocompatibility, biodegradability, non-toxic nature and distinct
physicochemical properties.
 Considering past success in various applications ranging from biomedical to
environmental, microbial biomaterials attract considerable attention from
biotechnologists. They are high-value products, and market value will grow
due to therapeutic and industrial potential.
 Your essay should describe and discusses different types of
biomaterials produced using bacteria, and the recent advances in
applications of these biomaterials. You should also comment on their
commercial viability.

Your answer should include:
 What is a biomaterial?
 Biopolymers and biomaterials
 Examples of functional biomaterials produced by bacteria
 Bacterial polyhydroxyalkanoates (PHAs) used as biomedical materials
for use medical implants, and drug delivery devices.
 Microbial cellulose—the natural power to heal wounds
 Polygamma glutamic acid – versitile bacterial biomaterial
 Importance of bacterially produced composites
 Advantages and disadvantages of ‘bacterial-engineered ‘
biomaterials in medical and environmental applications
 Conclusions
